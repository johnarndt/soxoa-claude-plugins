# Screenshot moment

Most products already contain one moment a customer would show to someone else. It is usually buried: behind a form, below the fold, cropped away from the brand, or gone after payment. This skill finds that moment and clears the path to it. It does not invent a new one.

## Inputs

- The product or service to review, with access to the flow (a public URL, a staging account, or the code).
- Who the customer is and where they would share: a group chat, Slack, LinkedIn, a family text. The destination changes what the moment should look like.

## Workflow

1. **Walk the flow** as a new customer, from first visit to first result. Note every screen where something specific to *them* appears: their name, their data, their words, a result about their business.
2. **Name the moment.** Pick the single screen where the reaction would be "look at this." Describe it in one sentence the way the customer would say it to a friend. It must be real and computed honestly; never propose staging a fake result.
3. **Have a challenger argue.** A second pass (a separate agent or a deliberate rethink) tries to pick a different moment or a different share destination. Keep whichever argument is stronger and note the disagreement.
4. **List what gets in its way**, checking each:
   - **Gate:** is the moment behind an email form, a card, an allowlist or a hidden toggle?
   - **Crop:** if someone screenshots just the interesting part, does the brand or URL survive?
   - **Share:** is there a share button, a copy-link, a download, or a native share sheet?
   - **Preview:** what does the link preview (title, image, description) show when the link is pasted into a phone chat? A generic card loses the moment.
   - **Proof:** does the image explain itself? If a result could look hand-made, it should say how it was produced.
   - **Persistence:** is the moment still there after payment or on a second visit?
5. **Propose fixes** and rank by leverage: people who reach the moment × how much the fix helps ÷ effort. Each fix names the screen or file and what changes.
6. **Deliver** one page per product: the moment, the challenger's view, the blockers, the ranked fixes. Build only what the owner picks.

## Rules

- Real data only. Never add fake counts, fake friends' activity or staged results to make something more shareable.
- Respect privacy. A shareable card must never expose another customer's data, and the person sharing must see exactly what will be shared.
- Mark anything you inferred from code or screenshots rather than saw running as unverified.
