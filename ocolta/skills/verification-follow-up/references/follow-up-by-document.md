# Follow-up by document type

Pick the items that fit the document and the open question. Work down the ladder: re-check, better copy, source verification. Stop when the material facts are confirmed. Contact any institution only under the authorization your policy and jurisdiction require.

## Bank statements

Re-check:
- Recompute opening balance + deposits - withdrawals against the reported closing balance, and any running balances.
- Compare the statement period with the dates on the transactions and with other documents you hold.
- Check whether the file passed through a portal, scanner, e-signature tool or PDF combiner, any of which can rewrite metadata legitimately.

Better copy:
- The same statement downloaded from the bank's own website or app, sent untouched (not printed, scanned, screenshotted, re-saved or merged).
- The month before or after, to test that the opening balance carries forward from the prior closing balance.
- The full statement rather than a summary page, if transaction detail is missing.

Verify at the source:
- A consumer-permissioned bank data connection the applicant authorizes (read-only, revocable by them).
- Or confirmation from the bank, with the applicant's written authorization, using the bank's published customer line or branch directory. Many banks confirm only limited facts; record exactly what they would and would not confirm.

## Paystubs

Re-check:
- Gross pay minus listed deductions against net pay.
- Rate times hours against gross, for hourly pay.
- Year-to-date totals against the pay date and pay frequency (a mid-year raise, bonus, retro pay or commission cycle can explain a mismatch).
- Whether deposits on a bank statement show a payroll provider's name instead of the employer's; that is common and ordinary.

Better copy:
- The stub as downloaded from the payroll provider's employee portal, untouched.
- An adjacent pay period, to test year-to-date continuity.

Verify at the source:
- A permissioned payroll connection or an income-verification service the employer already uses, authorized by the applicant.
- Or confirmation with the employer's HR or payroll office, with written authorization, using the company's published main number or a business registry. If a staffing firm or professional employer organization is involved, employment and payroll may be confirmed by two different parties.

## Employment and income letters

These carry the least internal structure, so source verification does most of the work.
- Find the employer independently (published main line, state business registry, directory). Do not use the phone number, email or web address on the letter.
- Confirm only what the decision needs: current employment, start date, title, pay rate or annual income, as your authorization allows.

## Neutral request to the applicant (draft)

```
Hi <name>,

Thanks for sending your <document>. To finish our review, could you send <specific request, e.g. "the <month> statement downloaded directly from <bank>'s website or app, without printing or editing it">?

If that is hard to get, let us know and we can look at another option, such as <alternative, e.g. "a secure read-only bank connection you authorize" or "your written authorization for us to confirm with your employer">.

We ask for this from every applicant at this step. Please reply by <date>.

Thank you,
<reviewer name>
```

Rules for the message: name the document and the specific item, ask for a source document rather than an explanation, do not say what was flagged or what you suspect, give a reasonable deadline, and send it in writing so it is part of the record.

## Institution confirmation script (draft)

```
Hello, my name is <name> from <organization>. I have written authorization from <applicant> to confirm <specific facts>. I found this number on <where you found it>. Can you confirm <fact 1> and <fact 2>?
```

Record: who you reached, their role, how you found the contact, date and time, and exactly what they confirmed or declined to confirm.

## Never

- Ask for online banking or payroll usernames, passwords, one-time codes, or a screen-share of a logged-in account.
- Use contact details printed on the document in question.
- Treat a slow, anxious or defensive reply as evidence. A refusal to provide an easy source document means more than tone, and is still not proof.
- Make a decision on the Ocolta result alone, or treat it as a consumer report.
